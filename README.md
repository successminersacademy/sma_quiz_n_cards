# Success Miners Learning Platform 💜

A student-focused learning and class management tool built for **Success Miners**.

This platform helps students stay consistent, track their progress, and prepare effectively through structured practice, revision tools, and attendance monitoring.

---

## 🎯 Purpose

Success Miners is designed to support students by providing:

- **Class Attendance Tracking** – Monitor presence and consistency
- **Interactive Flashcards** – Quick revision of key concepts across subjects
- **Weekly Quizzes** – Regular practice and self-assessment
- **Mock Exams** – Exam-style preparation under timed conditions

The goal is simple: help every Success Miner stay accountable, revise smarter, and perform better.

---

## ✨ Current Features

### 📚 Interactive Flashcards
- Topic-based flashcards for focused revision
- Flip animation (Question ↔ Answer)
- Swipe navigation
- Category filters
- Progress tracker
- Mobile-friendly design

**Currently available:**
- Physics – Measurements & Instruments (50 cards)

More subjects and topics will be added over time.

## 🛠️ How to Use (Flashcards)

1. Open the live site (or open `index.html` locally).
2. Use the category buttons to filter topics.
3. **Tap** the card to flip between Question and Answer.
4. **Swipe** left/right (or use the arrow buttons) to move between cards.
5. Track your progress with the bar at the top.
